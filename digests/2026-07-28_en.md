#### Summary
Diffusion transformers for video generation suffer from attention bottlenecks caused by long token sequences. Sol-Attn introduces a training-free sparse attention approach that unifies dynamic routing, sparse computation, and approximation correction within a single online-softmax pass: critical KV blocks are selected by comparing block proxy scores against a threshold during online softmax, while proxy scores of unselected blocks are directly reused to approximate their contributions, avoiding explicit proxy-map materialization. Experiments on image and video generation tasks show Sol-Attn delivers 2.1× and 2.3× end-to-end speedups for video generation and editing, respectively, while preserving visual quality.

- 🎯 Single online-softmax pass unifies dynamic routing, sparse computation, and approximation correction; training-free
- 💡 Online block thresholding replaces top-k ranking, enabling dynamic budgets without materializing the proxy score map
- 💡 Proxy scores of unselected blocks are reused to approximate their attention contribution, reducing accuracy loss
- 📊 2.1× end-to-end speedup for video generation and 2.3× for video editing with preserved visual quality
