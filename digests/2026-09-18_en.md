#### Summary
Running 35B-class MoE models on consumer hardware (single 24GB GPU) is bottlenecked by weight memory: even at 4-bit the weights are 19.5GB, and naive SSD offloading fails because layer N+1's expert selection cannot begin until layer N's output is ready. Edge0 introduces a prerouter—a per-layer head that predicts the next layer's routing one token ahead—so that the predicted expert set equals the routed set and prefetch reads are fully pipelined with compute. An unmerged recovery LoRA, trained on the student execution path, recovers quality lost to int4 quantization and routing substitution. On a single 24GB machine, Edge0 serves a 35B MoE at 20 tok/s within 3 GiB of peak active memory, staying within a few points of its fp16 teacher across five public benchmarks.
