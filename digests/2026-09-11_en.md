#### Summary
Low-bit quantization of LLM attention modules incurs large errors and performance degradation. HyQuant proposes a hybrid-precision quantization framework that quantizes most attention states to low-bit formats while retaining vertical-line tokens and local sliding window states in high precision, selected via lightweight vertical-line-aware attention-pattern signals. In the Prefill stage, a hybrid-precision attention operator preserves these accuracy-critical regions; in the Decode stage, KV-cache compression is combined with fused KV dequantization and attention computation to improve memory and hardware efficiency. Across diverse tasks, models, and datasets, HyQuant achieves nearly lossless accuracy with a simple design requiring no complex calibration.

- 🎯 Hybrid-precision design: most attention states quantized to low-bit, vertical-line tokens and local sliding window kept in full precision
- 💡 Lightweight vertical-line-aware attention-pattern signals identify accuracy-critical regions with minimal overhead
- 🔧 Decode stage fuses KV dequantization with attention computation to improve memory and hardware efficiency
- 📊 Nearly lossless accuracy across diverse tasks, models, and datasets with an extremely simple, calibration-free design
