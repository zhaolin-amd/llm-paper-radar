#### Summary
Autoregressive LLMs suffer from sequential decoding, causing high inference latency and low GPU utilization; existing speculative decoding methods still rely on autoregressive drafting, limiting practical speedups. DFlash introduces a lightweight block diffusion model as the draft model, generating all draft tokens in a single parallel forward pass while conditioning on context features extracted from the target LLM to improve draft quality and acceptance rates. Across diverse models and tasks, DFlash achieves over 6x lossless acceleration and delivers up to 2.5x higher speedup than the state-of-the-art method EAGLE-3.

- 🎯 Replaces autoregressive drafting with a lightweight block diffusion model that generates tokens in one forward pass
- 💡 Draft model is conditioned on context features from the target LLM to raise acceptance rates
- 📊 Achieves over 6x lossless speedup across models and tasks; up to 2.5x faster than EAGLE-3
