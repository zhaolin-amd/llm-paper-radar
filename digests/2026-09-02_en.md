#### Summary
FP4 pretraining is destabilized by the narrow dynamic range of the E2M1 format; NVIDIA's Transformer Engine NVFP4 recipe compensates with current-tensor scaling, a randomized Hadamard transform (RHT), and BF16 final layers. #### Summary
Forward KL distillation improves both reasoning and factual recall during pre-training, but during mid-training it slows factual recall acquisition while still boosting reasoning—a stage-dependent asymmetry traced to differing teacher confidence across procedural vs. knowledge-intensive data and the student's evolving knowledge state. Switch Distillation addresses this by using teacher predictive entropy as a routing signal: applying KD on high-confidence tokens and falling back to cross-entropy elsewhere.
