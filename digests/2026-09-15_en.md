#### Summary
Attention in video Diffusion Transformers faces two deployment bottlenecks: value outliers with no fixed channel or spatiotemporal structure inflate quantization scales and degrade accuracy, while the FP32 exponential in softmax becomes the longest pipeline stage when low-bit Tensor Cores accelerate only the matrix multiplications. VC-Attention is a training-free framework that pairs V-Smooth — lightweight online clustering to reorder value tokens, quantizing only the residual after subtracting the block mean (recovered from the online softmax row sum at no extra cost) — with ExpCast-FP8, which maps log-domain scores directly to E4M3 probability codes via a single fused multiply-add, eliminating the FP32 exponential and format conversion. - 📊 Attention kernel speedup over BF16 FlashAttention-4: 1.46–1.59× on datacenter cards (B200/H200), 2.3–3.6× on workstation cards

#### Summary
KV cache memory for LLM serving grows and shrinks dynamically per request, causing severe fragmentation and redundant duplication that limit batch size and throughput. PagedAttention adapts classical OS virtual memory paging to manage KV cache in non-contiguous fixed-size blocks, achieving near-zero memory waste and enabling flexible KV cache sharing within and across requests. Built on PagedAttention, vLLM delivers 2–4× higher throughput than FasterTransformer and Orca at the same latency level, with larger gains for longer sequences, bigger models, and more complex decoding algorithms.

#### Summary
KV-cache eviction perturbs the conditional token distributions in autoregressive generation; this work studies how first-divergence timing and subsequent token mismatch jointly determine cumulative disagreement. An exact decomposition is derived under a stepwise maximal coupling: the expected mismatch fraction equals a first-mismatch contribution plus post-divergence exposure multiplied by its mismatch rate. Residual-branch conditional Monte Carlo provides unbiased joint estimates of occurrence, occupation, and window/tail contributions. Experiments on Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct over 288 documents show that post-divergence exposure accounts for 85–90% of four aggregate mismatch gaps, and SnapKV at 50% retention enters divergence later and less often than SnapKV-512 or recent-token retention, though post-divergence TV remains high.

- 💡 Residual-branch conditional Monte Carlo yields unbiased joint estimates of occurrence, occupation, and window/tail components; per-replicate variance dominated by total token loss
- 📊 Post-divergence exposure accounts for 85–90% of four aggregate mismatch gaps across 288 documents
