#### Summary
Running 35B-class MoE models on consumer hardware with a single 24GB GPU faces two obstacles: even at 4-bit the weights are 19.5GB, and naive SSD offloading fails because expert selection for layer N+1 depends on layer N's output, preventing early I/O. Edge0 introduces a prerouter—a lightweight per-layer head that predicts the next layer's expert routing one token ahead—so SSD reads are fully pipelined behind compute with no dropped experts. A recovery LoRA trained on the student routing path compensates for quality loss from both INT4 quantization and routing replacement. The resulting system serves a 35B MoE at 20 tok/s within 3GiB of peak active memory on a single 24GB machine, staying within a few points of the fp16 teacher across five public benchmarks; the framework, checkpoints, and adapters are open-sourced.
