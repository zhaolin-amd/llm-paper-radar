#### Summary
Serving 35B-class MoE models on consumer hardware is constrained by weight memory: even at 4-bit the model weighs 19.5 GB, and naive SSD offloading fails because expert selection for layer N+1 depends on layer N's output, leaving no time to prefetch reads. A recovery LoRA trained on the student path compensates for quality loss from both INT4 quantization and routing substitution. #### Summary
