#### Summary
In small LLMs with modern vocabularies, the softmax output head holds 15–30% of all parameters, yet standard PTQ pipelines keep it in fp16, causing nominal 2-bit models to store far more bits per weight than claimed. SoftWater formulates head quantization as a rate-distortion problem under KL divergence, with a second-order analysis exposing a class-aware geometry where quantization error is jointly weighted by feature covariance and per-class softmax curvature. #### Summary
This is the first systematic study of Massive Activations (MAs) in layer-interleaved Hybrid Linear Attention (HLA) LLMs, identifying two architecture-aligned morphologies: pre-attention spikes (PAS) that appear immediately before full attention layers, and inter-spike plateaus (ISP) that persist through intervening linear attention layers. The study spans five linear attention architectures, six hybridization configurations, five data domains, and open-source hybrid models ranging from 1.2B to 397B parameters. Controlled pretraining experiments show that full attention output gating strongly attenuates MA magnitudes without eliminating their layerwise organization, while removing GDN gates yields comparatively modest amplification. Mechanistically, PAS follows a localized write-sink-cancel process and ISP is consistent with delayed cancellation, with both accounts converging to the stable MA morphology of full attention LLMs at the dense-attention limit.
