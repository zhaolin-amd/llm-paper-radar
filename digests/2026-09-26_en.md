#### Summary
The magnitude of activation outliers does not directly determine quantization quality; what matters is their alignment with the quantizer's structure. PrismQuant introduces a quantizer-aware rotation framework that aligns the leading activation eigenspace with the constant group subspace of asymmetric grouped INT4, where affine offsets absorb energy in this subspace without widening the within-group range. Rotation design is formulated as Ky Fan trace maximization, yielding a provably optimal closed-form solution, and compact Householder transformations enable gradient-free construction. Under W4A4KV4, Llama-3.1-70B achieves 3.85 PPL and 72.46% average zero-shot accuracy (only 0.22 percentage points below FP16), while Llama-3.1-8B delivers 1.51× prefill and 1.22× CUDA Graph decode speedups with 56.34% lower decode peak memory.

#### 🧭 Why this paper
**KV cache compression** · topic 4/5 · practicality 4/5 · composite 8/10 · Distance-KV proposes exploiting the relative distance dimension to learn static KV retention patterns, with the core contribution being an offline-learned retention mask over the joint layer × head × relative-distance space, requiring no online importance scoring and directly reused at inference. The method is straightforward, with clear benchmark comparisons against KIVI/H2O/StreamingLLM on RULER 128K, achieving 65.4% KV memory savings and 1.66× decoding speedup on Llama-3.1-8B. Limitations: the largest tested model is only 8B (restricting topic_relevance to 4), and RULER is a long-context-specific benchmark rather than general evaluations like MMLU/GSM8K—though RULER is the standard benchmark for long-context KV compression. Overall, the algorithm is simple, calibration cost is low, and measured speedup numbers are provided, giving a practicality score of 4.

#### Summary
KV cache memory and decoding latency in LLM inference scale rapidly with context length. Existing KV cache compression methods selectively retain tokens based on importance scores or cross-head attention patterns, but overlook that retrieval capability varies substantially with relative distance even within the same attention head. Distance-KV learns a static KV retention pattern offline over the joint space of layers, attention heads, and relative distances, with the language model frozen and no online importance scoring required at inference time. Across three backbone models and four long-context benchmarks, Distance-KV exceeds the strongest compression baseline by up to 9.3 points on RULER at 128K; on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at 128K, it reduces KV cache memory by 65.4% and achieves a 1.66× decoding speedup over Dense.

- 📊 Outperforms the strongest compression baseline by up to 9.3 points on RULER at 128K across three backbone models
- 📊 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct at 128K: 65.4% KV cache memory reduction and 1.66× decoding speedup over Dense
- 💡 Identifies relative distance as a distinct structural dimension for KV cache compression, complementing token importance and per-head attention patterns
