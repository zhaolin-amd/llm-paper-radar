#### Summary
Existing speculative decoding methods still rely on autoregressive drafting, limiting parallelism and practical speedups. DFlash introduces a lightweight block diffusion model as the drafter, generating multiple draft tokens in a single forward pass and conditioning the draft model on context features extracted from the target LLM to boost draft quality and acceptance rates. DFlash achieves over 6x lossless acceleration across a range of models and tasks, delivering up to 2.5x higher speedup than the state-of-the-art method EAGLE-3.

- 🎯 Block diffusion drafter generates all draft tokens in one forward pass, eliminating sequential autoregressive bottleneck
- 💡 Draft model is conditioned on context features from the target model to improve acceptance rates
- 📊 Over 6x lossless acceleration across models and tasks; up to 2.5x faster than EAGLE-3
