#### Summary
On-policy distillation (OPD) requires that the teacher providing token-level supervision be the same model that generated the SFT reference demonstrations, but this teacher consistency condition is routinely violated when SFT data have mixed provenance or when different models are used for SFT generation and distillation. Lightning OPD 2.0 introduces cross-fitted style residualization, which uses rollout-level cross-fitting to estimate the recurring component of teacher–reference disagreement attributable to wording, formatting, and reasoning cadence differences, then subtracts this style bias estimate before constructing the token-level OPD update. On mathematical reasoning and code generation benchmarks, Lightning OPD 2.0 consistently outperforms Lightning OPD in cross-teacher settings, achieving 82.4% on AIME 2024 and 63.0% on LiveCodeBench v5 starting from Klear-Reasoner-8B-SFT. The approach relaxes teacher consistency as a prerequisite, allowing the SFT data generator and distillation teacher to be selected independently.
