#### Summary
Running 35B MoE models on consumer hardware (24GB VRAM) is bottlenecked by weight memory: 4-bit quantization still yields 19.5GB, and naive SSD offloading cannot hide I/O latency because expert selection for layer N+1 depends on layer N's output. Edge0 introduces a prerouter—a per-layer lightweight head that predicts the next layer's routing one token ahead, with the prediction used directly as the actual routing, enabling SSD prefetch to overlap with compute. A recovery LoRA trained on the student path compensates for quality loss from both INT4 quantization and routing replacement. On a single 24GB machine, Edge0 achieves 20 tok/s with only 3GiB of peak active memory, landing within a few points of its FP16 teacher across five public benchmarks.

#### Summary
Weight-activation quantization reduces inference memory and compute costs, but existing methods typically require expensive optimization and architecture-specific cross-layer basis-transform propagation. MACaQ is an MXFP4 W4A4 post-training quantization method that treats every linear layer independently: it applies a per-channel scale and block-Hadamard transform online to activations while absorbing the inverse transforms into weights, enabling a simple single-layer streaming implementation. A Hessian-aware coordinate-descent procedure called Flip-Refinement further improves over round-to-nearest by greedily moving individual FP4 codes to adjacent representable values. MACaQ surpasses DuQuant++ and MR-GPTQ in perplexity on Llama-3-8B while using 11× less quantization time, quantizes a 122B-parameter model in 64 minutes using only 32.5GB GPU RAM, and adds only 2.3% end-to-end overhead over pure MXFP4 on Qwen3-32B in vLLM.

- 🎯 Per-layer independent processing: online block-Hadamard transform + per-channel scale; inverse absorbed into weights, no cross-layer dependency
- 💡 Flip-Refinement: Hessian-aware coordinate descent greedily moves FP4 codes to adjacent representable values, outperforming round-to-nearest
- 📊 11× faster than DuQuant++/MR-GPTQ at matching perplexity on Llama-3-8B; 122B model quantized in 64 minutes with 32.5GB GPU RAM
- 🔧 Only 2.3% end-to-end overhead over pure MXFP4 on Qwen3-32B in vLLM; generalizes to image diffusion models without architecture-specific changes
