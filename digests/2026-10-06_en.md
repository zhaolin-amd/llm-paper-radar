#### Summary

#### Summary
Ternary language models (BitNet b1.58, Falcon-E, BitCPM) export latent weights by first casting to bf16 before threshold-based ternary quantization. This bf16 cast introduces rounding errors: when products land exactly on the quantization threshold, ties-to-even maps them to zero, causing 0.83–1.77% code disagreements in Falcon-E/BitCPM and 1.530% in BitNet 2B-4T relative to shipped checkpoints. #### Summary

#### Summary
ApexQuant is a calibration-free quantization method that iteratively re-quantizes the residual error of existing quantizers, acting as a refinement layer requiring no training data. The key insight is that applying a fresh random rotation to each residual returns it to a uniform distribution on the hypersphere, which enables a closed-form prediction—before reading any weights—of how many passes are needed to reach a target weight-space error. Because each prefix of passes is itself a valid lower-rate model, a single quantization artifact supports multiple precisions. - 📊 Result: within a few percent of full precision at 4-bit; best two-bit performance measured; fully data-free, including for remote-sensing and medical domains
