#### Summary
In PD disaggregation, the KV cache becomes a network payload, and existing reduction methods are binary (selected tokens transmitted at full precision, others dropped). SpectrumKV instead assigns per-token precision: FP16 for attention sinks and high-importance tokens, INT8 for medium, INT4 for low when the model tolerates it. Since INT4 tolerance is model-dependent (Qwen2.5-7B fails catastrophically while Mistral-7B and Gemma-2-9B remain stable), a deployment-time probe of three NIAH trials selects a 3-tier or 2-tier policy.
