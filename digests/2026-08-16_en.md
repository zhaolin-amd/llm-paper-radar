#### Summary
Weight-only PTQ suffers accuracy collapse at 2-3 bits because existing backpropagation-free optimizers ignore the correction absorbable by a continuous suffix and keep the quantization grid fixed. SCHUROPT analytically eliminates the suffix's optimal continuous response via Schur complement, reducing the problem to an exact groupwise quadratic, then alternates closed-form row-wise scale/zero-point refitting with coordinate descent over integer codes. #### Summary
Binary quantization promises extreme compression for LLMs but existing methods fail to fully exploit acceleration due to reliance on floating-point arithmetic or runtime dequantization. FluxBin addresses this through algorithm-kernel co-design: algorithmically, it introduces Decoupled Row-Column Binary Decomposition with Hessian-guided saliency-aware hybrid bases; at the kernel level, it implements a LUT Building Approach with Scale Fusion and Virtual Columnar Mapping to convert sparse salient matrices into dense execution. Across diverse model architectures, FluxBin achieves up to 5.92× speedup and 10.19× energy savings, enabling 70B-scale model deployment on a single A100 GPU with 4× memory reduction while matching the accuracy of heavily fine-tuned methods.

#### Summary
Deploying large AI models on resource-constrained edge devices demands compression, but strong compression results do not guarantee good deployment outcomes. This paper surveys dozens of recent works reporting compression on real hardware and derives practical deployment guidelines, then empirically tests them on GPU, CPU, and Raspberry Pi across question answering and image segmentation. For QA, Qwen3.5 0.8B under Q5_K_M GGUF quantization achieves 93.85 SQuAD F1 and 92 EM, while structured pruning at the same precision costs 16 F1 points at a 1% ratio; the ranking reverses for segmentation, where pruning cuts model size by ~80% at near-constant mIoU. Critical failure modes are identified: pruning can break k-quant super-block alignment, inflating deployed artifacts by 21–49% and raising Raspberry Pi latency up to 3.4x, and a LoRA-recovered model can appear competent at 71% strict BoolQ accuracy while collapsing to single-class prediction (52.6% balanced accuracy).

- ⚠️ LoRA-recovered model holds 71% strict BoolQ accuracy yet routes 97/100 predictions to one class; balanced accuracy drops to 52.6%
