#### Summary
LLM inference under long-context and long-form reasoning workloads is increasingly bottlenecked by HBM capacity due to the dominance of KV cache. OasisKV decouples full KV-cache storage from HBM by offloading it to host or remote memory, using lookahead tokens drafted by speculative decoding to predict which KV blocks will be important in the next decode step, and prefetching them via a background attention pipeline before they are needed. Under a 2,048-token KV budget, accuracy stays within 0.7 points of full attention; throughput improves 1.69× on reasoning workloads and up to 2.1× on multi-GPU long-context serving over dense vLLM. Under prefill–decode disaggregation, OasisKV achieves ~2× dense throughput while reducing KV transfer by 6.5–9.7× and decode-node host memory by 2.2–2.6×.

- 📊 Under prefill–decode disaggregation: ~2× dense throughput, 6.5–9.7× less KV transfer, 2.2–2.6× less decode-node host memory
- 🔧 Implemented on vLLM; no offline calibration required — KV importance predicted dynamically at runtime
