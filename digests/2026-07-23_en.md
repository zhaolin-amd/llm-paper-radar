#### Summary
W4A4 PTQ of diffusion transformers degrades output quality because activation outliers cannot be represented in 4-bit formats. The standard remedy applies an invertible transform to activations online at every denoising step, creating a computational bottleneck: per-channel scaling (SmoothQuant) sacrifices accuracy, fixed Hadamard transforms require large blocks with high cost, and learned full-rank transforms require a prohibitive d×d GEMM per layer per step. KroQuant applies a learned Kronecker-structured invertible transform to each 32-element activation block, storing fewer than half the parameters of per-channel scaling, executing as small tensor-core GEMMs online, and absorbing residual per-weight quantization error via offline LoRaQ calibration. On PixArt-Σ, SANA, and FLUX.1-schnell at W4A4 (MXFP4e2), KroQuant produces outputs closer to the FP reference than SVDQuant and LoRaQ on MJHQ-30K and SDCI, with the quantizer kernel running up to 14% faster than SmoothQuant on an MI350 GPU.
