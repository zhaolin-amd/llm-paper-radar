#### Summary
MoE inference on consumer hardware is bottlenecked by weight memory: a 35B model still occupies 19.5GB at 4-bit, and sparsity only reduces compute per token, not bytes. Naive SSD offloading fails because expert reads for layer N+1 cannot begin until layer N's output is available, leaving no compute to hide behind. Edge0 introduces a prerouter—a per-layer head that predicts the next layer's expert routing one token ahead and uses that prediction as the actual routing decision—enabling fully overlapped streaming prefetch from SSD. A recovery LoRA trained on the student path compensates for quality loss from both INT4 quantization and routing replacement; on a single 24GB GPU, Edge0 serves a 35B MoE at 20 tok/s within 3GiB of peak active memory, staying within a few points of the fp16 teacher across five public benchmarks.

- 🎯 Prerouter predicts next-layer expert routing one token ahead, fully overlapping SSD reads with compute
- 📊 35B MoE runs at 20 tok/s on a single 24GB GPU with only 3GiB peak active memory, near fp16 quality on five benchmarks
- 💡 Recovery LoRA trained on the student path jointly compensates INT4 quantization loss and routing-replacement error
- 🔧 Framework, checkpoints, and adapters are open-sourced; an 8B MoE tier runs on the same stack

#### Summary
Offline KV-cache compaction builds a smaller cache once from a fixed context and reuses it across repeated queries, but existing methods score each candidate entry independently, ignoring the nonlinear coupling introduced by softmax renormalization. KV-Surgeon uses calibration data to construct a quadratic surrogate that yields closed-form expressions for jointly determining the optimal greedy removal decision and compensating the remaining entries. On QuALITY, LongHealth, and Long Code Arena across dense and mixture-of-experts models from 4B to 30B parameters, KV-Surgeon advances the cost–accuracy Pareto frontier at every evaluated compaction ratio. At 50× compaction, it improves question-answering accuracy by up to four points over the state-of-the-art attention matching method at comparable cost, and a faster variant exceeds the state of the art while requiring only two fifths of its cost.
