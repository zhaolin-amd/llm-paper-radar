#### Summary
Single-GPU inference of 70B-parameter models is constrained by device memory, long-context throughput, and engineering integration cost. This work frames single-GPU inference as a budget-aware design problem across these three axes and studies how pruning, quantization, and KV-cache compression interact under realistic execution. Controlled ablations reveal that layer-wise pruning makes weight quantization more robust, KV-cache sparsification complements INT8 KV quantization, and static vector quantizers conflict with dynamic caching. Guided by these coupling findings, the authors assembled a pipeline that compresses a 70B model to ~33 GB, sustains ~57 tokens/s on 10k-token prompts on a single A40, and keeps accuracy within 5% on common and reasoning benchmarks.
