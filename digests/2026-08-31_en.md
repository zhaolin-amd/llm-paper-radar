#### Summary
Hybrid linear-attention models (e.g., Kimi-Linear, Qwen with GDN) require storing recurrent state checkpoints alongside full-attention KV caches for prefix reuse, increasing memory pressure and causing frequent evictions. DASC analyzes the decay structure of GDN and KDA to derive per-head/per-channel retention horizons directly from model weights, then selects long-horizon state units and packs them into a ragged checkpoint layout, discarding short-horizon units.
