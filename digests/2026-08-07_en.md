#### Summary
Existing KV cache compression methods apply fixed, predefined rules and focus separately on token eviction or merging, preventing flexible resource sharing across layers, heads, and context slots or joint balancing of local resolution and information coverage. GraceKV formulates KV cache compression as a global resource allocation problem under a fixed cache budget, treating each layer–KV head–slot combination as an atomic unit organized into a prototype tree where leaf nodes are token-level KV entries and internal nodes use a single prototype to compress their subtree. Expanding coverage adds new tree roots while improving resolution splits selected nodes; all candidate actions compete globally for the shared budget. Without any additional training and with the entire process running on GPU, GraceKV ranks first in 24 of 32 long-context task and compression-ratio settings and remains robust up to 128-fold compression.
