#### Summary
Autoregressive LLMs suffer from sequential decoding, causing high latency and poor GPU utilization; while speculative decoding helps, existing drafting stages remain sequential. DFlash introduces a lightweight block diffusion model as the drafter, generating multiple draft tokens in a single forward pass conditioned on context features extracted from the target model, achieving higher acceptance rates. Experiments demonstrate over 6x lossless acceleration across diverse models and tasks, delivering up to 2.5x higher speedup than the state-of-the-art EAGLE-3.

- 🎯 Lightweight block diffusion drafter generates multiple tokens in a single forward pass, replacing autoregressive drafting
- 💡 Draft model is conditioned on context features extracted from the target LLM to boost acceptance rates and output quality
- 📊 Achieves over 6x lossless speedup across models and tasks, up to 2.5x faster than EAGLE-3
- ⚠️ Requires training a new lightweight block diffusion draft model, incurring non-trivial training cost
