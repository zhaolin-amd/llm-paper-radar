#### Summary
Serving 35B-class MoE models on consumer hardware is memory-bound: 4-bit quantization still requires 19.5GB, and expert sparsity reduces compute per token but not the bytes that must be accessed. Naive SSD offloading fails because layer N+1's expert selection depends on layer N's output, preventing early I/O initiation to hide latency behind compute. #### Summary
