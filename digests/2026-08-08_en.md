#### Summary
Multi-turn ReAct agents accumulate growing KV caches as trajectories expand, and existing attention-score-based eviction methods cannot distinguish temporarily dormant tokens from those that have genuinely completed their role. CommitKV partitions completed agent events into token pages and performs paired measurements of each page's deletion effect both before a tool-call commit and after the returned observation is incorporated. A greedy joint test accepts pages for retirement only when their combined post-commit effect remains within a bound; at subsequent compression checkpoints, awaiting-measurement pages are protected, retired pages are excluded, and remaining KV states are kept within the cache budget with consistent key/value/position indices. Experiments across various benchmarks show CommitKV reduces agent memory use and end-to-end latency while achieving higher accuracy than existing KV cache compression methods.

- 💡 Greedy joint test retires candidate pages only when their combined post-commit deletion effect stays bounded

- 📊 Outperforms existing KV cache compression baselines on various benchmarks in memory, latency, and accuracy (exact figures not disclosed)
