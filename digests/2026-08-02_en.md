#### 🧭 Why this paper
**KV cache compression** · topic 4/5 · practicality 3/5 · composite 7/10 · RestoreKV is a practical enhancement for query-agnostic KV cache eviction that compensates for eviction-lost information by generating a compact restore cache from a small number of restore tokens during the prefill stage, orthogonally compatible with existing eviction methods. The method uses LoRA adapters (only 0.4% of parameters), is trained via self-distillation, and disables adapters for all subsequent queries at inference time with negligible overhead. Effectiveness is validated on long-context benchmarks such as RULER, improving 59/60 settings across five base eviction methods on Qwen3-4B, demonstrating strong practical utility. However, the largest tested model is only 4B, and the accuracy benchmarks are relatively narrow (only RULER and KVPress, without general benchmarks like MMLU/GSM8K), leading to a topic_relevance of 4 rather than 5. Regarding practicality, while LoRA training is lightweight, it still requires a training step and must be paired with specific eviction methods, resulting in a practicality score of 3.

#### Summary
Query-agnostic KV cache eviction reuses a single compressed cache for all future queries, but accuracy collapses under tight budgets; existing approaches focus on better KV pair selection. RestoreKV complements selection with learned restoration under the same total KV budget: after prefill, a small set of restore tokens attend to the full KV cache in a single LoRA-adapted forward pass, producing a compact, context-conditioned restore cache that recovers eviction-lost information. The base eviction rule remains unchanged, LoRA adapters are disabled for all subsequent decoding, and training via self-distillation updates only 0.4% of parameters with no task-specific tuning. On Qwen3-4B, RestoreKV improves 59 of 60 budget-matched settings across five base eviction methods; at a 5% budget it raises KVzip from 38.2 to 73.2 on RULER-4K, and applied to KVzip+ it achieves 86.4 RULER accuracy at 16× compression with less than 0.5% cache-construction overhead.

- 💡 Self-distillation trains only 0.4% of parameters, no task-specific tuning needed; <0.5% cache-construction overhead in 32K-context evaluation
- 📊 Validated across four backbones and four long-context benchmarks; 59/60 paired budget-matched settings improved across five base eviction methods
