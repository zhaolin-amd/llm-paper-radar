#### Summary
Serving 35B-class MoE models on consumer hardware is bottlenecked by weight memory (19.5GB at 4-bit), and naive SSD offloading fails because expert selection for layer N+1 depends on layer N's output, making early prefetching impossible. Edge0 addresses this with a prerouter: a lightweight per-layer head predicts the next layer's routing one token ahead, and its prediction is used directly as the routing decision, ensuring the staged expert set equals the routed set and hiding SSD read latency behind compute. Quality loss from int4 quantization and routing replacement is recovered by an unmerged LoRA adapter trained on the student (prerouter-driven) inference path.
