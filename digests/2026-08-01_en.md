#### Summary
KV cache memory costs grow prohibitively with context length in LLMs, and existing low-rank compression methods face a dilemma: offline approaches require external calibration data, while online approaches pay heavy compute for full-prompt decomposition. S⁴R builds low-rank key/value subspaces from selectively sampled tokens via prompt-aware initialization, eliminating external calibration dependence while controlling prefill cost. During decoding, sparse reconstruction retains only informative positions, avoiding the throughput penalty of full cache reconstruction at every step. Experiments on LongBench and RULER with Llama and Qwen model families demonstrate up to 5× KV compression with near full-cache accuracy.
