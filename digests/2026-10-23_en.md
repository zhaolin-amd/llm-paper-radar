#### Summary

#### Summary
Running 35B-class MoE models on consumer hardware is constrained by weight memory: at 4-bit quantization the model requires 19.5GB, and naive SSD offloading fails because expert weights for layer N+1 cannot be prefetched before layer N produces its routing decision. On a single 24GB machine, Edge0 serves a 35B MoE at 20 tok/s within 3GiB of peak active memory, matching its fp16 teacher within a few points across five public benchmarks.

#### Summary
Existing PTQ solvers such as GPTQ, LDLQ, and block coordinate descent commit each weight block to a single codeword before neighboring blocks are considered, corresponding to a zero-temperature hard-decision limit. MPQ recasts row-wise PTQ as posterior inference over discrete codebooks, using vector approximate message passing (VAMP) to propagate soft posterior information across blocks before committing to hard codewords at the output stage. A block coordinate descent step further refines the hard-decision initialization. Evaluated on 2-bit lattice and 3/4-bit scalar quantization across Llama, Qwen, and Mistral model families, MPQ improves perplexity and zero-shot accuracy over strong hard-decision baselines and composes well with activation-aware scaling and incoherence processing.
