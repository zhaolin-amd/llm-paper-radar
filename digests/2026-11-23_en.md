#### Summary
Running 35B-class MoE models on consumer hardware is blocked by weight memory: at 4-bit quantization the model still occupies 19.5GB, and naive SSD offloading fails because layer N+1's expert routing cannot be known before layer N finishes, preventing early reads. #### Summary
