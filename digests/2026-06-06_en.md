#### Summary
Targets the KV-cache memory bottleneck in long-horizon test-time scaling decoding. Shows that under autoregressive decoding, quantization errors accumulate across timesteps, mainly driven by incorrect token scales — a regime missed by prefill-style evaluations. Proposes KVarN, a calibration-free quantizer combining a Hadamard rotation with dual-axis variance normalization on K and V. At 2-bit precision it sets a new KV-cache quantization SOTA on MATH500, AIME24 and HumanEval, with a vLLM implementation released.

- 💡 Identifies cross-timestep error accumulation in autoregressive KV quantization
- 🎯 Hadamard rotation + dual-axis variance normalization on K/V, calibration-free
- 📊 New SOTA on MATH500, AIME24, HumanEval at 2-bit KV-cache
- 🔧 vLLM implementation released for long-horizon reasoning
