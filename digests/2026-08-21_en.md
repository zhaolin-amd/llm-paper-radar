#### Summary
Autoregressive LLMs suffer from sequential decoding, causing high inference latency and poor GPU utilization. DFlash introduces a speculative decoding framework that replaces the conventional autoregressive draft model with a lightweight block diffusion model, generating all draft tokens in a single forward pass conditioned on context features extracted from the target model. This parallel drafting approach improves draft quality and acceptance rates. DFlash achieves over 6x lossless acceleration across diverse models and tasks, delivering up to 2.5x higher speedup than the state-of-the-art method EAGLE-3.

- 🎯 Replaces autoregressive drafter with a lightweight block diffusion model generating draft tokens in one forward pass
- 💡 Conditions the draft model on context features from the target LLM to boost output quality and acceptance rate
- 📊 Achieves >6x lossless speedup across models and tasks; up to 2.5x faster than EAGLE-3
- ⚠️ Requires training a dedicated lightweight block diffusion draft model before deployment
