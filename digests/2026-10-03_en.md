#### Summary
MoE inference on consumer hardware is memory-bound: a 35B-class model occupies 19.5GB at 4-bit, and sparsity reduces compute but not the bytes that must be loaded. Naive SSD offloading fails because the next layer's expert selection cannot be known early enough to prefetch ahead of compute. Edge0 introduces a prerouter that predicts each layer's routing one token in advance, enabling fully pipelined SSD reads; the predicted routing is used directly as the actual routing so no experts are dropped. A recovery LoRA trained on the student path compensates for both int4 quantization loss and routing replacement error. On a single 24GB machine, Edge0 serves a 35B MoE at 20tok/s within 3GiB of peak active memory, within a few points of the fp16 teacher across five public benchmarks.
