#### 🧭 Why this paper
**KV cache compression** · topic 4/5 · practicality 4/5 · composite 8/10 · PACE performs 4-bit quantization of the recurrent state in linear-attention/hybrid models, which is analogous to KV cache compression (fixed-size state buffer compression). The method has two core contributions: (1) rounding only once every few tokens (periodic rounding), leveraging existing chunked kernel mechanisms to reduce accumulated error; (2) column-aligned quantization scales, with error-sensitive heads kept at 8-bit. Evaluated on four hybrid models from 4B to 35B parameters, compared against existing state-quantization baselines DAMP and Q-Mamba. #### Summary
Linear-attention language models maintain a fixed-size recurrent state per decoding sequence; quantizing this state to lower precision allows more sequences to share an accelerator. Existing state quantizers round after every generated token and suffer notable accuracy loss at 4 bits without fine-tuning or increased bit width. PACE introduces two complementary techniques: (1) periodic rounding — updating the quantized state only once every few tokens (mirroring the chunk-level rounding already used in prefill chunked kernels) with intermediate tokens held in a small buffer, reducing accumulated quantization error; and (2) column-aligned scales that exploit the observation that large entries in the gated-delta state concentrate in specific channels, plus retaining 8-bit precision for the heads that accumulate the longest-lasting rounding error. Evaluated on four hybrid models from 4B to 35B parameters, PACE with mixed 4/8-bit heads matches 8-bit DAMP on the three larger models at roughly two-thirds its memory, PACE with all 4-bit heads outperforms Q-Mamba at equal memory, and periodic rounding preserves the decoding speedup of the buffer approach.

- 🎯 Periodic rounding (once per few tokens) + column-aligned quantization scales reduce recurrent-state error without any fine-tuning
- 📊 Mixed 4/8-bit head variant matches 8-bit DAMP at ~2/3 its memory on three larger models (up to 35B)
- 📊 All-4-bit head variant outperforms Q-Mamba at equal memory budget
- 🔧 Periodic rounding is compatible with existing chunked kernels and preserves decoding speedup

#### Summary
