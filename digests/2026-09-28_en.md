On Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at 50% expert pruning, SlimWise achieves up to 1.81x decode throughput improvement with minimal accuracy loss.

- 🎯 Prefill uses full model; decode uses pruned model with training-free KV cache handoff, no format conversion needed
- 📊 Up to 1.81x decode throughput on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B at 50% expert pruning with minimal accuracy loss
- 💡 Shows benchmark accuracy can conceal pruning-induced generation-length distortions
- 🔧 Fully implemented in vLLM, supporting both PD-disaggregated and PD-colocated serving

#### Summary
In MoE language model expert offloading, decoding speed is bottlenecked by the number of experts fetched per token. MaskCoFT introduces a learnable binary mask during fine-tuning that restricts Top-K routing to an expert subset, while jointly training routers and experts with cross-entropy loss so experts adapt to redirected tokens. At inference, the mask becomes a soft prior that re-ranks experts without hard exclusion, keeping all experts selectable. On Mixtral-8x7B, expert fetches are reduced by 23.7% and time-per-output-token by up to 16.4%; on DeepSeek-V2-Lite, by 10.1% and 5.5%, respectively, with average accuracy over nine benchmarks exceeding the base model by 0.92 and 0.53 points.

- 📊 DeepSeek-V2-Lite: 10.1% fewer expert fetches/token, up to 5.5% lower time-per-output-token, +0.53 avg accuracy over 9 benchmarks vs. base model
- 💡 Inference-time soft prior re-ranks but never eliminates experts, requiring no kernel modification to the offloading system
