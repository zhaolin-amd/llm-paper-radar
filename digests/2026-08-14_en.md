#### Summary
PTQ degrades rapidly at low bit-widths, and standard QAT suffers from a gradient mismatch between latent full-precision weights and their lossy quantized reconstructions, raising the loss floor. QUASAR integrates lightweight, loss-aware reconstruction into every QAT training step: it uses the exponential moving average of squared gradients as online saliency estimates, searches over a small set of clipping ranges, and fits affine dequantizers via saliency-weighted least squares. Theoretical analysis shows that the loss-aware reconstruction error is the sole reconstruction-dependent term in the QAT convergence bound, making QUASAR's objective a principled optimization target. #### Summary

#### Summary
Massive Activations (MAs) in hybrid linear attention (HLA) LLMs have not been systematically studied prior to this work. Two architecture-aligned MA morphologies are identified: pre-attention spikes (PAS) that appear immediately before full attention layers, and inter-spike plateaus (ISP) that persist across intervening linear attention layers; as full attention density increases, successive PAS become connected via ISP, eventually recovering the stable MA morphology of full attention LLMs. The analysis spans five linear attention architectures, six hybridization configurations, five data domains, and open-source hybrid models from 1.2B to 397B parameters. Controlled pretraining of GDN-based hybrids up to 1.3B shows that full attention output gating strongly attenuates MA absolute magnitudes without disrupting layerwise organization, while removing GDN gates causes comparatively modest amplification; a shared lifecycle account based on MA cancellation timing explains both morphologies.

- 🎯 First systematic characterization of two MA morphologies in HLA LLMs: pre-attention spikes (PAS) and inter-spike plateaus (ISP)
- 📊 Study covers five linear attention architectures, six hybrid configurations, and models ranging from 1.2B to 397B parameters
- 💡 Full attention output gating strongly suppresses MA magnitudes but preserves layerwise organization; explained by a write-sink-cancel lifecycle with timing differences
- ⚠️ Purely measurement/characterization work; no new quantization algorithm or downstream benchmark (MMLU/GSM8K) evaluation
