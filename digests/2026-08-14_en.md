#### Summary
Ternary LLMs such as BitNet b1.58 lack efficient edge inference support. Bitnet.cpp targets the dominant bottleneck—mixed-precision GEMM (mpGEMM)—with a dedicated library featuring two kernels: Ternary Lookup Table (TL), which fixes spatial inefficiencies of bit-wise methods, and Int2 with a Scale (I2_S), which guarantees lossless inference. On CPU edge hardware, Bitnet.cpp achieves up to 6.25x speedup over full-precision baselines and up to 2.32x over low-bit baselines. TL is further generalized to an element-wise lookup table (ELUT) for broader low-bit LLM applicability.

- 🎯 TL and I2_S kernels target mpGEMM in ternary LLMs, enabling sub-2-bits-per-weight lossless inference
- 📊 Up to 6.25x speedup vs FP16 baseline and 2.32x vs low-bit baseline on CPU edge inference

#### Summary
Massive Activations (MAs) in layer-interleaved hybrid linear attention (HLA) LLMs have not previously been studied systematically. Across five linear attention architectures, six hybridization configurations, five data domains, and open-source models spanning 1.2B to 397B parameters, this work identifies two architecture-aligned MA morphologies: pre-attention spikes (PAS) that arise immediately before full attention layers, and inter-spike plateaus (ISP) that persist through intervening linear attention layers. Controlled pretraining of GDN-based hybrids up to 1.3B shows both morphologies emerge early, with full attention output gating strongly attenuating absolute magnitudes without eliminating layerwise organization, while removing GDN gates yields only modest amplification. Mechanistically, PAS follows a localized write-sink-cancel process and ISP corresponds to delayed cancellation, with the full attention limit recovering the stable MA morphology of standard full attention LLMs.

- 🎯 First systematic study of MAs in HLA LLMs; identifies PAS and ISP morphologies across models from 1.2B to 397B parameters
- 📊 Consistent organization reproduced across 5 linear attention architectures, 6 hybridization configs, and 5 data domains
- 💡 Full attention output gating strongly suppresses MA magnitudes but preserves layerwise structure; removing GDN gates causes only modest amplification
- 💡 Unified lifecycle: PAS follows localized write-sink-cancel; ISP reflects delayed cancellation converging to full-attention MA morphology
