#### Summary
Knowledge distillation (KD) for small language models is memory- and compute-intensive. This work presents two systems-level optimizations: (1) offline KD, which caches the teacher's top-K logits once and removes the teacher from GPU memory, achieving ~29% faster iterations and up to 41% higher throughput on a single H200 GPU while matching online KD training loss; (2) a fused, chunked KL loss that avoids materializing the full vocabulary-sized logit tensor, reducing peak memory to linear in sequence length and enabling training at 32,768 tokens on a single GPU. A separate toy benchmark validates memory and iteration-rate scaling of the loss kernel from 4K to 256K tokens.

- 🎯 Offline KD caches teacher top-K logits once, removing teacher from memory; ~29% faster per iteration, up to 41% higher throughput on a single H200 GPU
- 💡 Fused chunked KL loss keeps peak memory linear in sequence length, enabling 4× context (32,768 tokens) training on a single GPU
- 📊 Toy benchmark confirms memory and iteration-rate scaling of the loss kernel from 4K to 256K tokens
- ⚠️ Evaluation limited to training loss and scaling curves; no downstream benchmarks (e.g., MMLU, HumanEval) reported
