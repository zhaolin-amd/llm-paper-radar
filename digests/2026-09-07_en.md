#### Summary
Structured pruning recovery suffers from a capacity-knowledge asymmetry: the recovery module lacks representational capacity to re-absorb knowledge from removed components. OverRep addresses this by temporarily overparameterizing the recovery module during training, using knowledge distillation from the original model to fill the representational gap, then algebraically merging the overcomplete module into a mathematically equivalent compact form with unchanged inference-time architecture and cost. An annealed activation function enables nonlinear training dynamics while converging to a linear regime that permits exact algebraic merging. Across three backbone families, OverRep improves retained reasoning performance over strong recovery baselines by up to 5.5 and 8.4 points at 25% and 50% pruning, with memory and TFLOPs comparable to existing recovery methods.

- 🎯 Overcomplete reparameterization during training is algebraically merged into a compact module, adding zero inference overhead
- 💡 Annealed activation enables nonlinear training dynamics and converges to a linear regime for exact algebraic folding
- 📊 Up to 5.5 and 8.4 point gains on reasoning benchmarks at 25% and 50% pruning across three backbone families
- 🔧 Memory and TFLOPs remain on par with existing recovery methods; code is open-sourced

#### Summary
Zero-shot task accuracy relies solely on argmax predictions and is insensitive to distributional changes introduced by quantization, exhibiting non-monotonic behavior that masks fidelity loss relative to the BFloat16 baseline. A distribution-sensitive evaluation framework is proposed that computes Jensen-Shannon Divergence and Total Variation Distance between full-precision and quantized models' full-vocabulary predictive distributions at the token decision boundary. A 120-run experimental matrix spanning five foundation architectures, four reasoning benchmarks, and quantization regimes from BF16 to Q2_K shows that divergence metrics generally increase monotonically with quantization strength, and that mixed-precision Q4_K yields lower divergence than uniform Q4_0 at similar memory footprints.
