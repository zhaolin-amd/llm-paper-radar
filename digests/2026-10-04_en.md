#### Summary
MoE inference on consumer hardware faces a memory wall: a 35B-class model is 19.5 GB at 4-bit, and sparsity reduces compute per token but not the bytes that must be loaded. Edge0 introduces a prerouter—a per-layer head that predicts the next layer's expert routing one token ahead—so SSD reads can be fully overlapped with the current layer's compute and no experts are dropped. A recovery LoRA, trained on the student routing path, compensates for quality loss from both int4 quantization and routing replacement. On a single 24 GB machine, Edge0 serves a 35B MoE at 20 tok/s within 3 GiB of peak active memory, staying within a few points of the fp16 teacher across five public benchmarks.
