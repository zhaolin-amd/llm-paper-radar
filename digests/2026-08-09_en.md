#### Summary
Efficient edge inference for ternary LLMs such as BitNet b1.58 has remained underexplored. Bitnet.cpp addresses this by providing an inference system centered on a novel mpGEMM library that enables sub-2-bits-per-weight, lossless inference through two kernels: Ternary Lookup Table (TL), which overcomes the spatial inefficiency of prior bit-wise methods, and Int2 with a Scale (I2_S), which guarantees lossless edge inference. Experiments demonstrate up to 6.25x speedup over full-precision baselines and up to 2.32x over low-bit baselines. The system is publicly released in the Microsoft BitNet repository.

- 🎯 TL and I2_S mpGEMM kernels enable sub-2-bits-per-weight lossless ternary LLM inference
- 📊 Up to 6.25x speedup vs. full-precision baselines; up to 2.32x vs. low-bit baselines
- 💡 TL resolves spatial inefficiency of bit-wise methods; I2_S ensures lossless edge deployment
- 🔧 Open-sourced in the Microsoft BitNet repository for practical CPU/edge deployment
