#### Summary

#### Summary
In agentic sessions running to a million tokens with many sessions resident simultaneously, the KV cache and ranking index reside in host memory, and the full-key scan for top-k selection becomes the bandwidth bottleneck. Fathom introduces per-query variable-depth bit-plane scanning: the 4-bit K cache is stored channel-major as bit planes, so reading t planes exactly implements a t-bit quantizer per channel; each query allocates its bit budget across channels via reverse water-filling weighted by channel variance-based importance. - 📊 18% fewer bytes than SparQ 68-bit (r=16) with lower attention error in 6/7 settings; coding-agent step agreement of 136-bit scan achieved at 92 bits
- ⚠️ Gains apply only when KV cache is offloaded to host memory; no benefit when the index is GPU-resident

#### Summary
Long spatiotemporal sequences in Diffusion Transformer video generation make attention the dominant deployment cost, requiring low-bit kernels that are both accurate and fast. Accuracy is bottlenecked by unstructured value outliers, and speed by the FP32 exponential inside softmax. VC-Attention is a training-free low-bit attention framework that pairs V-Smooth—online clustering to reorder value tokens so they quantize well together, with the block mean subtracted and restored from the online softmax row sum—with ExpCast-FP8, which maps log-domain scores to E4M3 probability codes via a single fused multiply-add, eliminating the FP32 exponential entirely. Across Wan2.2, HunyuanVideo-1.5, LongCat-Video, and MiniMax-H3, the attention kernel runs 1.46–1.59× faster than BF16 FlashAttention-4 on datacenter Blackwell/Hopper GPUs and 2.3–3.6× faster on workstation cards, with end-to-end speedups of 1.13–1.19× and 1.36–1.70× respectively.

- 🎯 V-Smooth reorders value tokens via lightweight online clustering and quantizes the residual after subtracting the block mean, recovered from the online softmax row sum
- 🎯 ExpCast-FP8 replaces the FP32 exponential with a single fused multiply-add mapping log-domain scores directly to E4M3 probability codes
- 📊 Attention kernel is 1.46–1.59× faster than BF16 FlashAttention-4 on B200/H200, and 2.3–3.6× faster on RTX PRO 6000/RTX 5090
- 📊 End-to-end video generation is 1.13–1.19× faster on datacenter GPUs and 1.36–1.70× faster on workstation cards
