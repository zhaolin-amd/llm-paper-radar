#### Summary
Reasoning traces can accompany correct answers without providing effective supervision for a specific student model. ReDiST constructs student-conditioned traces by starting from the student's own attempts: answer-correct drafts are revised against supplied evidence, while unsuccessful drafts are repaired with guidance from a verified answer. A frozen student-matched reader screens and ranks candidates using answer recovery and verified-answer probability, and the selected traces are distilled with an auxiliary answer loss balancing trace and final-answer supervision. Experiments on QA benchmarks show improvements over answer-only, original-trace, and trace-selection/rewriting baselines on both source-task and cross-benchmark transfer.

- 🎯 Student-conditioned trace construction revises answer-correct drafts against evidence and repairs failed drafts using verified answers
- 💡 Frozen student-matched reader ranks trace candidates by answer recovery rate and verified-answer probability
- 🔧 Construction and selection are fully offline; inference uses only the distilled student model
- 📊 Outperforms answer-only, original-trace, and trace-rewriting baselines on both source and transfer QA benchmarks
