#### Summary

This work reframes compressed checkpoints as initializations that benefit from a small post-compression adjustment stage. Across 2 MoE LLM backbones, 4 pruning/merging methods, 3 expert-retention ratios, and 28 benchmarks, LM fine-tuning and teacher-based KD are compared under matched small-data budgets with measured GPU costs. Using only 3,000 C4 examples for a single epoch, Full FT recovers on average 37.3% of the original-to-compressed performance gap, with LM fine-tuning proving more cost-effective than standard token-level KD.
